# Rushes

**Your shoots. Your projects. One organized system.**

Less lost. More made.

Rushes is an open-source media manager being built for filmmakers, photographers, and production teams. Find video, photos, and audio, bring in new shoots, and keep your archive organized—on storage you control.

## Why Rushes?

You remember the shoot. You don’t always remember the filename—or which drive it ended up on.

Rushes is designed for the media collection you already have: years of shoots, camera-card copies, old projects, external drives, and shared storage. It brings organization, ingest, search, and archive maintenance into one app.

## Three workspaces

- **Search:** Find media by shoot, filename, folder, or tag. Browse video, photos, and audio together with useful previews and details.
- **Ingest:** Choose a source, name the shoot, confirm its destination, and follow copying and verification progress.
- **Manage:** Organize your media and keep your storage healthy. Plan folder rules, review duplicates, identify rebuildable editing files, and see which jobs need attention. The workspace title is **Media Management**.

## A home for every part of production

| Area | What belongs here |
| --- | --- |
| Archive | Original video, photos, and audio, organized by your departments, clients, or projects, then year, date, and event |
| Projects | Editing projects, graphics, and finished exports |
| Library | Reusable logos, fonts, templates, and music |

Labels let media belong to more than one category without creating extra copies.

## Your storage. Your call.

The design keeps media on your own hardware, with no cloud account, media uploads, or tracking. A local owner-created password protects maintenance actions. Cleanup moves files into a holding folder for review; space is reclaimed only when that folder is deliberately emptied.

Today Rushes runs on a NAS with a helper on a Mac. Self-contained Mac and Windows applications are planned and do not exist yet. See the application documentation for setup details.
